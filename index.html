ASCENT: Advances in Systems and Computation at the Edge and HPC Networks

The 2nd workshop on HPC, edge computing, LLM-enabled systems, generative AI, quantum-aware workflows, and distributed intelligent infrastructure.

Call for Papers ICDCN 2027
WhenDuring ICDCN 2027, 4-7 January 2027
WhereNational Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal, India

About the Workshop

ASCENT 2027 brings together researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of high-performance computing, edge systems, AI, and emerging computing paradigms. The workshop is designed as a focused forum for architectures, algorithms, systems, and applications that push sophisticated computation closer to where data is produced.

Edge-HPC Systems

Scalable, fault-tolerant infrastructure for decentralized and resource-constrained environments, including placement, orchestration, scheduling, and performance optimization.

AI and LLMs

Distributed inference, model compression, privacy-preserving learning, generative AI services, and AI accelerator management across edge-HPC systems.

Quantum-Aware Workflows

Quantum computing, quantum-inspired optimization, and hybrid quantum-HPC-edge workflows for complex distributed applications.

Call for Papers

We welcome original research, forward-looking ideas, practical experience reports, and use-case-driven studies across the converging ecosystems of HPC, edge computing, AI, LLMs, and quantum computing.

  • Architectures for the edge, fog, and cloud continuum
  • HPC-inspired solutions adapted for edge environments
  • AI, generative AI, and LLMs on HPC-powered edge platforms
  • Distributed LLM inference, serving, compression, and orchestration
  • Data management, caching, and consistency across edge nodes
  • Security, privacy, and trust in decentralized systems
  • Energy efficiency and sustainability for edge HPC
  • Resource orchestration and multi-tenancy across the edge
  • 5G/6G, O-RAN, VLC, mmWave, and network-aware edge computing
  • Quantum computing, quantum-inspired optimization, and hybrid workflows
  • IoT, smart grids, transportation, agriculture, AR/VR, and public safety
  • Benchmarking, testbeds, and large-scale experimental deployments

Submission Guidelines

Prepare your manuscript for double-blind review using the official ACM proceedings format. Key publication and submission requirements are organized below.

ICPS

Proceedings & Open Access

Accepted workshop papers will appear in a companion volume with the ICDCN 2027 proceedings through the ACM International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS), indexed by ACM, DBLP, Scopus, and other indexing services.

Under ACM's ICPS open-access model, an Article Processing Charge may apply when an author's institution is not part of ACM Open and the author does not qualify for a waiver.
