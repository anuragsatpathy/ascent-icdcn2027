# ASCENT 2027 Google Sites Build Guide

This guide is for creating the Google Sites version of:

**ASCENT: 2nd Workshop on Advances in Systems and Computation at the Edge and HPC Networks**

Reference sources used:

- Previous ASCENT edition: https://sites.google.com/view/ascent-icdcn2026/
- ICDCN 2027 conference site: https://cs3-lab.github.io/icdcn/index.html
- Proposal file: `IDCDN_27_Workshop_Proposal.docx`

## Site Settings

**Site name:** ASCENT 2027

**Navigation tabs, in order:**

1. Home
2. Committee
3. Program
4. Keynote
5. Previous Edition

For the final tab, choose **Pages > New link** in Google Sites and use:

`https://sites.google.com/view/ascent-icdcn2026/`

**Recommended Google Sites theme:**

- Theme: Simple or Aristotle
- Accent color: teal or deep green
- Font style: Clean / modern
- Header type on Home: Cover
- Header type on internal pages: Large banner

### About the Workshop

ASCENT 2027 brings together researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of high-performance computing, edge systems, AI, and emerging computing paradigms. The workshop is designed as a focused forum for architectures, algorithms, systems, and applications that push sophisticated computation closer to where data is produced.

Use three cards or three columns:

**Edge-HPC Systems**

Scalable, fault-tolerant infrastructure for decentralized and resource-constrained environments, including placement, orchestration, scheduling, and performance optimization.

**AI and LLMs**

Distributed inference, model compression, privacy-preserving learning, generative AI services, and AI accelerator management across edge-HPC systems.

**Quantum-Aware Workflows**

Quantum computing, quantum-inspired optimization, and hybrid quantum-HPC-edge workflows for complex distributed applications.

### Call for Papers

Use a two-column section with text on the left and `assets/nitk-main-building.jpeg` on the right.

**Section intro:**

We welcome original research, forward-looking ideas, practical experience reports, and use-case-driven studies across the converging ecosystems of HPC, edge computing, AI, LLMs, and quantum computing.

**Topics of interest:**

- Architectures for the edge, fog, and cloud continuum
- HPC-inspired solutions adapted for edge environments
- AI, generative AI, and LLMs on HPC-powered edge platforms

- Data management, caching, and consistency across distributed edge nodes
- Security, privacy, and trust in edge-based or decentralized systems
- Energy efficiency and sustainability for edge HPC
- Privacy-preserving and energy-efficient deployment of LLMs at the edge
- Resource orchestration and multi-tenancy across the edge
- Network-aware and adaptive edge computing, including 5G/6G, O-RAN, VLC, and mmWave
- Resource management for AI accelerators, LLM workloads, and quantum-enabled workflows
- Quantum computing, quantum-inspired optimization, and hybrid quantum-HPC-edge systems
- Applications in IoT, smart grids, transportation, agriculture, AR/VR, and public safety systems at the edge
- Autonomous decision-making at the edge, including self-healing and self-optimization
- Benchmarking, testbeds, and large-scale experimental environments for edge HPC
- Economic and incentive models for edge-HPC ecosystems
- Challenges of edge-HPC in extreme environments, including underwater, underground, and aerial networks
- Heterogeneous architectures for edge HPC
- Performance modeling, analysis, and prediction of applications at high-performing edge systems
- Scientific algorithms for hybrid and heterogeneous architectures
- Efficient AI/ML approaches for edge HPC
- Performance evaluation of emerging AI accelerators, including GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, and neuromorphic architectures

### Submission Guidelines

All submissions must use the official ACM LaTeX article template `acmart.cls`, version 1.80 or greater, with the following document class instruction:

`\documentclass[sigconf,authordraft]{acmart}`

Papers must not exceed 6 pages, double-column, including figures, tables, and references, in standard ACM format. Papers should not be published or under review elsewhere.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for ICDCN at the full conference rate and present the work in person at the workshop.

All papers must be submitted through the EasyChair portal.

## Gmail / Google Sites Publishing Steps

1. Go to https://sites.google.com/new while signed into the Gmail account that should own the site.
2. Create a blank site.
3. Set the site name to **ASCENT 2027**.
4. Add the pages in this order: Home, Committee, Program, Keynote.
5. Add **Previous Edition** as a linked page using the URL above.
6. Upload the three images from the `assets` folder.
7. Build each page using the copy blocks in this guide.
8. Use **Preview** to check desktop and mobile layouts.
9. Click **Publish** and choose a URL such as `ascent-icdcn2027`.
10. In **Share with others**, add collaborators by Gmail address if needed.

Google Sites publishing must be completed while signed into the target Gmail account. I cannot authenticate into your Gmail account or directly publish the Google Site from this environment.
